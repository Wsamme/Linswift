Why Readers Struggle With Dense Academic Prose

A meticulous researcher documented every anomaly in the manuscript, yet novice readers still found the convoluted phrasing intimidating.

Because the terminology was unusually ornate and the argument remained implicit for several paragraphs, newcomers had to infer the writer's intention from sparse contextual clues rather than from any concise explanation.

Their resilience improved over time, but the article remained demanding enough that even diligent students highlighted unfamiliar expressions before they could summarize the central claim with confidence.
